### 00 / ABOUT

I turn operational problems into software people actually use. I like working across the whole journey: understanding a routine, modeling the data, building the business rules, and designing an interface that makes sense.

My foundation is **PHP and Laravel**; on the frontend, I work with **Vue, React, and TypeScript**. I also handle integrations, deployment, and the evolution of production systems. I'm the founder of **Appruma**, a product in development for independent professionals.

> **What drives me:** code that leaves the repository and improves a real operation.
